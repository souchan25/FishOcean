About FishCurrent

Bridging Communities, Empowering Fisherfolk

Our Mission

FishCurrent was created to directly connect fisherfolk from Kabankalan and Ilog with customers who value fresh, locally-sourced seafood. We eliminate middlemen, ensuring fair prices for sellers and the freshest catches for buyers.

The Story

In coastal towns like Kabankalan and Ilog, hardworking fisherfolk wake up before dawn to bring in the day's catch. Yet many struggle to reach customers directly, relying on intermediaries who take a significant cut.

FishCurrent changes that. Our platform gives every seller a digital storefront, chat functionality to build customer relationships, and tools to manage inventory—all for free.

For buyers, it means knowing exactly where your fish comes from, chatting with the person who caught it, and supporting local economies with every purchase.
